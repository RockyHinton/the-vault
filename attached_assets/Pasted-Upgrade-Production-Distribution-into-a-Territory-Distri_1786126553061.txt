Upgrade Production → Distribution into a Territory Distribution Workspace

Replace the current generic document upload page with a dedicated Distribution Workspace designed for film territory management.

This should follow the existing Vault design language while feeling spacious, premium and easy to navigate. Avoid dense layouts. Large cards, generous spacing and clear typography are more important than fitting lots of information on screen.

UX

Maintain the existing Vault styling.

Requirements:

generous whitespace

smooth transitions
consistent spacing
rounded corners matching existing design
no cramped tables
no SaaS-style spreadsheet layouts

Hover effects should only include:

slight lift

subtle border highlight

Nothing flashy.

Behaviour

Deleting a note removes it permanently.

Deleting a document removes it permanently.

Deleting a territory removes all associated notes and documents after confirmation.

Documents and notes belong only to their territory.

Scope

Only replace the Production → Distribution page.

Do not modify any other Production pages.

Do not add royalty tracking, payment schedules, contract automation or analytics yet.

The goal of this iteration is to transform Distribution from a generic file upload page into a clean, production-ready territory management workspace that producers will naturally use during film distribution, while leaving room for future expansion into full licensing and royalty management.
